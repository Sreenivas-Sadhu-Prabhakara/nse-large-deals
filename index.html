Did trading get more profitable in the AI era?

Five years of large-deal desks, split at the launch of ChatGPT and marked with every major AI model since. The honest answer: total profits rose — but almost entirely because desks traded far more, not because each trade earned more. And the AI dates? They sit on a trend that was already underway.

04 / THE HONEST VERDICT

More profit — but not because the machines got smarter

What the data actually says

Yes, desks made more money in the AI era — about 2.7× the monthly profit of the pre-ChatGPT years. But the extra money came purely from trading 3.4× more, not from a better edge. Profit grew slower than volume (2.7× vs 3.4×), so on a like-for-like basis each rupee traded actually earned a touch less — the edge eased from 9.8 to 7.7 bps. And that gentle squeeze was already underway before ChatGPT. The AI launch dates are drawn on the timeline as markers; they line up with no visible break in either profit-per-rupee or the trend. This is the long arc of algorithmic competition and a 2023–25 bull market — not evidence that AI models moved these profits.

05 / READ BEFORE YOU QUOTE

Honest caveats

Why this is a proxy, and why AI ≠ cause

  • “Profit” is a proxy. It's only same-day round-trips in disclosed bulk/block deals (>~0.5% of a company). Real desk P&L — carried positions, undisclosed trades, fees, borrow — isn't here. Treat totals as a floor.
  • The ChatGPT date is not special. Volume rises and edge eases smoothly with calendar time, so any cut date in 2022–23 would produce the same “after > before” jump. Splitting exactly at ChatGPT doesn't make it the cause.
  • Correlation, not causation. Nothing here proves AI models moved these profits. A 2023–25 bull market, new HFT desks entering, and rising disclosure all push the same way and can't be separated out.
  • Composition changes. Big desks like Junomoneta only appear from 2025, and average deal size swings year to year. Rising volume partly reflects new entrants, not the same desks doing more.
  • The trend predates ChatGPT. The edge was already easing across 2020–2022, so the post-2022 picture just continues an existing arc — there is no structural break at the AI launches.
  • 2026 is a partial year (through 15 Jul) — don't compare its total to full years.
Method. For every (house, day, stock) with both a buy and a sell, realized round-trip P&L = (avg sell − avg buy) × matched quantity; edge = P&L ÷ matched value, in basis points (winsorized ±500 bps). 38,452 round-trips over 2020-01-01 → 2026-07-15. Source: public NSE/BSE bulk & block deal disclosures. AI launch dates: ChatGPT 30-Nov-2022, GPT-4/Claude Mar-2023, Gemini Dec-2023, GPT-4o May-2024, DeepSeek-R1 Jan-2025.
